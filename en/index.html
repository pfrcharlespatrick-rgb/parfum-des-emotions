The Perfume of Emotions

The client describes what they feel; the perfumer receives a composition sheet — raw materials, olfactory pyramid and indicative dosages — to start working from.

1Tell us

A memory, a place, a person, a moment this perfume should hold. Write freely: the words are read and translated into materials.

2Choose your emotions

Two to four are enough. The emotions inferred from your story are already suggested.

3Set the material

No knowledge of perfumery is needed: follow your instinct.

4Specify the use

When will it be worn, and what is out of the question?

To set aside:

Percentages are expressed within the concentrate and given as guidance only: they are a laboratory starting point, not a finished formula. IFRA compliance, allergen control and the actual balance of the blend remain the perfumer's work and responsibility.
